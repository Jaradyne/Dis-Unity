# Current evidence packet — 2026-09-22

For current-event claims, treat **2025-01-01 through 2026-09-22** as the working current-evidence horizon. Older material below is explicitly background only. This packet is a sensor bundle for agents without live search; it is not canonical state.

## Diesel / distillate

- EIA's September 2026 Short-Term Energy Outlook says U.S. distillate inventories are forecast to fall below 100 million barrels in September and remain below the 2021-2025 five-year low through much of 2027. EIA attributes tight domestic conditions partly to a tight global distillate market and elevated exports. Source: https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/report/
- Reuters on 2026-09-21 reports global diesel prices at record highs; U.S. refineries are operating near their highest utilization in years, U.S. stockpiles remain about 15% below the five-year seasonal average, and few quick supply-expansion options remain. Source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/global-diesel-prices-hit-record-highs-further-rises-possible-2026-09-21/
- Reuters on 2026-09-21 reports industry analysts and storage data indicating global diesel tightness is likely to extend into 2027; this is analysis, not certainty. Source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/global-diesel-shortage-likely-last-into-2027-storage-tanks-drain-2026-09-21/
- EIA reports 2025 U.S. transportation-sector petroleum-diesel consumption of about 2.94 million barrels/day, approximately 123 million gallons/day, excluding biodiesel and renewable diesel. Diesel also powers much U.S. farm/construction equipment and many backup generators. Source: https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/diesel-fuel/use-of-diesel.php
- Reuters reports the ExxonMobil Joliet, Illinois refinery (275,000 b/d; roughly 11 million gallons/day of gasoline plus diesel production capability) remained offline after a September power/flooding incident, illustrating how one refinery outage compounds an already-tight system. Source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/exxon-mobil-says-floodwater-overwhelmed-pump-at-joliet-illinois-refinery-2026-09-17/
- On 2026-09-17 the U.S. temporarily relaxed hours-of-service rules for drivers hauling gasoline and diesel, citing short-term distribution delays and cost pressure. Source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/us-temporarily-eases-curb-working-hours-fuel-truck-drivers-2026-09-17/

## Agriculture and farm finance

- Reuters on 2026-09-18 reports record diesel prices striking U.S. farmers during harvest, with examples of daily fuel costs doubling and some farmers using older gasoline tractors. It also reports concern about independent-trucker bankruptcies and delayed pass-through to food prices. Source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/record-us-diesel-prices-squeeze-farmers-food-prices-may-rise-2026-09-18/
- USDA ERS May 2026 forecast: farm-sector debt rises 5.2% in 2026 to $624.7B; debt is forecast to grow faster than assets and sector solvency/liquidity worsen. Source: https://www.ers.usda.gov/topics/farm-economy/farm-sector-income-finances/assets-debt-and-wealth/
- USDA NASS 2024 TOTAL results released March 2026: 348M acres of farmland are rented; 79% of rented acres are owned by non-farming landlords. About 43M acres are expected to transfer ownership in the next five years, with about 23M expected to be sold to non-relatives. This does not establish corporate consolidation by itself, but creates a useful baseline for monitoring who acquires distressed land. Source: https://www.nass.usda.gov/Newsroom/2026/03-12-2026.php
- USDA reports complex farm bankruptcies had increased over the preceding year in its 2026 explanatory notes, but bankruptcy counts and causes must be checked directly rather than inferred from diesel alone. Source: https://www.usda.gov/sites/default/files/documents/16-2026-CJ-OGC.pdf
- Agricultural bankruptcy monitoring should include Chapter 12 and agriculture-related Chapter 11 filings, creditor sales, auction outcomes, operator exit, land ownership changes and who continues to operate the land. Avoid assuming an acquisition motive without evidence.

## Farming cannot simply replace modern machines with hands

- Iowa State 2026 field-capacity estimates put an 8-row corn combine near 8.7 acres/hour, a 12-row around 12.8 acres/hour and a 16-row around 16-17 acres/hour under typical assumptions. Source: https://www.extension.iastate.edu/AGDM/crops/pdf/a3-24.pdf
- FAO background data estimates manual maize harvest at about 120-200 person-hours/hectare, before additional hand shucking; this is historical/international background rather than a current U.S. labor forecast. Source: https://www.fao.org/4/t0522e/t0522e05.htm
- Implication: extra hands can rescue bounded labor tasks, sorting, gleaning, repair, irrigation, loading, local transport and some specialty crops, but cannot immediately replace combines/tractors across broadacre U.S. acreage. Fuel prioritization, equipment sharing, alternative fuels, older gasoline equipment and reducing nonessential machine work are more plausible first-line substitutions.

## Rail

- AAR reports a U.S. freight network near 140,000 route-miles, six Class I railroads and roughly 615 short lines. Class I carriers operate about 67% of mileage; short lines are crucial connectors for rural industries. Source: https://www.aar.org/freight-rail-facts-figures/
- FRA explicitly identifies deteriorating rural rail infrastructure and funds short-line bridges, multimodal connections and other rural improvements. Source: https://railroads.fra.dot.gov/rural
- FRA's current CRISI program has about $2.039B available under the FY25-26 NOFO for eligible rail safety/capacity/short-line/intermodal projects. Source: https://railroads.fra.dot.gov/grants-loans/consolidated-rail-infrastructure-and-safety-improvements-crisi-program
- Rail is not an independent escape from diesel: most U.S. freight locomotives are diesel-electric. Its value is moving much more freight per gallon and concentrating truck use on first/last mile. Rail resilience also has concentration risks; Chicago alone handles roughly one in four rail carloads/intermodal units according to AAR.
- Towns without rail, navigable water or cheap trucking need explicit local analysis: distance to transload points, bridge/road condition, gasoline/EV light-vehicle capacity for priority loads, local storage, cooperatives, postal/package networks, food production, healthcare cold-chain requirements and reciprocal routes.

## Biodiesel / renewable diesel

- DOE AFDC: all OEMs approve B5; many modern diesel engines approve B20. B20 or lower can generally be used without modification, but warranties should be checked. B100 requires special handling, has cold-weather/storage/material-compatibility issues, and is usually used as blendstock. Sources: https://afdc.energy.gov/fuels/biodiesel-blends and https://afdc.energy.gov/vehicles/diesel
- Renewable diesel is chemically equivalent to petroleum diesel and can be a drop-in fuel; biodiesel is chemically different. Source: https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/biofuels/biodiesel-rd-other-use-supply.php
- EIA June 2026 operable capacity: biodiesel 1.955B gal/year; renewable diesel and other biofuels 4.969B gal/year. These are capacities, not guaranteed spare output and the latter category includes products other than renewable diesel. Source: https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/pet_pnp_capbio_dcu_nus_m.htm
- Commercial on-road biodiesel producers must register fuel with EPA and provide process/raw-material information and an ASTM D6751 laboratory report; other fuel-program registrations may apply. Source: https://www.epa.gov/fuels-registration-reporting-and-compliance-help/am-i-required-register-biodiesel-how-would-i-do
- EPA warns biodiesel process wastewater/glycerin/methanol streams can create severe wastewater and fire/explosion hazards if mishandled. Small emergency production is therefore not merely a matter of buying a reaction vessel. Source: https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=P100282R.TXT
- Current federal grant reality: USDA announced in March 2026 that REAP would make no further grant awards until revised regulations are effective; guaranteed loans continue. Thus a new entrant should not be modeled as receiving an immediate existing REAP grant today. Source: https://www.rd.usda.gov/alert/stakeholder-announcement-rural-business-cooperative-service-update-status-rural-energy-american
- USDA's Section 9003 biorefinery program is currently open but offers loan guarantees, not a no-match startup grant, and requires substantial expertise/capital structure. Source: https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/energy-programs/biorefinery-renewable-chemical-and-biobased-product-manufacturing-program
- Historical background only: Auburn's university-scale system processed about 3,300 gal/year of waste vegetable oil using a $17,000 processor; older economic models for a 10M gal/year plant estimated about $11.3M construction cost in mid-2000s dollars. These are useful scale anchors, not 2026 quotes.

## Medical cold chain and pandemic lens

- CDC's 2026 vaccine-storage guidance treats cold-chain integrity and emergency storage/transport planning as essential. Source: https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/storage-handling/
- WHO's refrigerated-vehicle standards state small vans/trucks may power refrigeration from the vehicle engine, while larger trucks/semitrailers often have independent diesel-powered refrigeration units; electrical backup may be available when parked. Source: https://extranet.who.int/prequal/immunization-devices/e002-refrigerated-vehicles
- CDC guidance includes generator/battery backup and alternative storage planning for power failures. Pandemic or emergency conditions can therefore couple diesel availability to both transport and backup electricity for temperature-sensitive medical supplies.

## Evidence discipline for this packet

- Current facts above have dated sources where available.
- News analysis about how long the crunch will last is not certainty.
- A local outage or viral report proves only the bounded event after verification, not national prevalence.
- Other-model output is not evidence. Gemini should use this packet to reason, challenge and propose research, not invent fresh September 2026 facts.
