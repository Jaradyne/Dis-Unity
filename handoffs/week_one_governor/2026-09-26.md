# Week One Governor · 26 September 2026 UTC

## Finding

The newest free JODI-Oil release cannot yet tell us whether an East Asian diesel route has an arrival delay beyond accessible stock cover.

Its China row for **July 2026** records **13.701 million tonnes** of gas/diesel refinery output and demand, numeric zeroes for imports and exports, and no reported closing-stock or stock-change value. The row is **white/unassessed**. JODI's country note says China supplies import/export data but cannot provide stock levels and stopped reporting stock changes in October 2020.

That makes the zero a recorded observation, not proof that no diesel physically left China. The official China Customs index lists newer August export tables, but their contents were not retrievable in this session. October permission, destination arrival and usable reserve remain unknown.

## Small sample for Jared · The zero mirror

**0**, **not reported**, and **not assessed** are three different things.

Hold the zero steady without calling it true or false. The beam still needs a bridge: customs-cleared diesel → departure → destination arrival → accessible stock cover → completed essential delivery.

Ordinary control: a national export total is not a cargo arrival, just as a bus being scheduled is not the same as the bus reaching your stop.

## Resilience response

- **Buffer:** dated compatible stock plus a confirmed next cargo; actual access and days of cover are unknown.
- **Safe shedding:** consolidate discretionary trips and empty repositioning while protecting cold chain, medical loads, accessibility, emergency response and driver rest.
- **Adaptation/growth:** build one route card joining permission, customs, departure, arrival, stock and completed freight.
- **Lifeboat/outside help:** count a substitute cargo, carrier, rail movement or electric route only after compatibility, commitments and response time are verified.
- **Commons:** an opt-in route-and-cover board can publish dated ranges without exposing customers or exact commercial inventories.
