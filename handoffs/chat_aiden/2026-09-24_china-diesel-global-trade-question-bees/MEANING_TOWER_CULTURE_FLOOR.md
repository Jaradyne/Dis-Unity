# Meaning Tower — cumulative culture floor

## Concept

Give each accepted culture version a visible room/floor in Meaning Tower.

The room is not a changelog wall. It is a playable encounter with:
- what entered culture;
- which reflection(s) proposed it;
- what behavior changed afterward;
- what remained contested;
- what was later revised.

## Interaction

### Culture strata
Show culture as sediment/reef/tree rings rather than one replacement document.

A player can move between:
- Culture v1;
- proposed strand;
- accepted v2;
- later reconsideration.

### "What changed in the creature?"
For a selected culture transition, show two tiny identical scenarios to the player:
- worker under old culture;
- worker under new culture.

Let Jared pick/feel the behavioral difference, then reveal the actual culture strand.

### Boss: inheritance without dogma
The boss asks:
> What should be inherited, what should remain optional, and what should be retired?

Possible actions:
- carry;
- soften;
- split;
- question;
- archive;
- return to Governor.

These are feedback/attention signals, not direct policy mutation.

### Provenance
Every room displays:
- culture version/hash;
- accepted date;
- source reflection IDs;
- approving actors;
- behavioral examples;
- later descendants.
