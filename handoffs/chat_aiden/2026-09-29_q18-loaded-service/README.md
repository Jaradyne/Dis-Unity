# Q18 — loaded-service evidence after the nervous-system build

Base: main after PR #30 (`6e07fa4dbb23b17d4dfe9e3642accd02ba015163`)

Question: **Which local diesel function can be replaced with demonstrated reliable service?**

Status: **new public evidence materially advances the question, but does not yet establish dependable long-run service.**

## What changed

The previous Q18 packet was right to separate **delivery of electric hardware** from **dependable freight service**. Fresh public research now finds several later operating records that move the evidence ladder forward:

1. **Ningyuan Diankun entered the Ningbo–Jiaxing/Zhapu commercial route in mid-April 2026.**
2. By 15 June, Ningbo-Zhoushan Port's own company-news surface described the vessel as having run the route for **more than a month** and showed it departing again toward Jiaxing.
3. A Zhejiang provincial government information surface, sourcing Zhejiang Daily, reports the sister vessel **Ningyuan Dianpeng left Jiaxing fully loaded with containers** for Ningbo-Zhoushan Port on the zero-carbon sea-river intermodal route.
4. The same provincial source reports Diankun's **first month of operation saved 67 tonnes of fuel and about 168 tonnes of CO2**, with higher annual savings presented as projections.
5. Ningbo Ocean Shipping's current company profile says **both vessels entered commercial-route operation in April and June** and are operating at scale on established routes.

That is no longer merely a construction/delivery story.

It is evidence of:
- real route entry;
- repeated operation over at least a month for Diankun;
- at least one explicitly **loaded** Dianpeng voyage;
- reported realized fuel displacement for Diankun's first operating month.

It still does **not** provide the reliability series Q18 originally asked for: scheduled vs actual departures/arrivals, completed-voyage count, uptime/downtime, charger/swap availability, cancellations, payload by voyage, or audited diesel displacement over a longer defined interval.

So the bounded update is:

> **Electric coastal container service has crossed from delivered hardware into documented loaded and repeated commercial operation. Dependable service is now plausible and partially demonstrated, but long-run reliability remains unmeasured in the sources reviewed here.**

## Claims

### FACT — route operation
By June 2026, a Ningbo-Zhoushan Port corporate news surface described Diankun as having operated the Ningbo Ocean Shipping Zhapu route for more than a month and departing again toward Jiaxing.
Evidence: CHAT-Q18-01.

### FACT — loaded commercial movement
A Zhejiang government information surface reports Dianpeng departing Jiaxing fully loaded with containers for Ningbo-Zhoushan on the newly launched sea-river zero-carbon route.
Evidence: CHAT-Q18-02, CHAT-Q18-03.

### FACT ABOUT REPORTED OPERATING RESULT
The Zhejiang source reports that Diankun's first month of operation saved 67 tonnes of fuel and about 168 tonnes of CO2.
Evidence: CHAT-Q18-02, CHAT-Q18-03.
Boundary: this packet has not independently audited the calculation or denominator.

### PLAUSIBLE MECHANISM / PARTIAL DEMONSTRATION
Repeated operation plus an explicitly loaded sister-vessel voyage and a reported first-month fuel saving are substantially stronger evidence of diesel-function replacement than vessel delivery alone.
Evidence: CHAT-Q18-01 through CHAT-Q18-04.
Boundary: this still does not quantify service reliability.

### UNKNOWN
Long-run dependable freight service remains unmeasured here. Missing denominator:
- scheduled voyages;
- completed voyages;
- cancellations/delays;
- charger/swap uptime;
- maintenance downtime;
- tonnes/TEU per voyage;
- diesel backup/substitution events;
- audited fuel displacement over a defined period.

## CROSS / lineage note

CHAT-Q18-01 and CHAT-Q18-04 are operating-company/port-family surfaces and should not be treated as fully independent of Ningbo Ocean Shipping's own reporting.

CHAT-Q18-02/03 are Zhejiang government information surfaces relaying different media/government-origin reports, but overlap heavily in the same route-launch facts. Count the underlying event once.

The key epistemic advance is therefore **not “many sources agree.”** It is that later dated material measures a different stage of the function:
delivery → maiden route entry → repeated operation → loaded movement → reported first-month fuel displacement.
