# Rhetorical Occlusion / Conversation Accessibility

## Observation

Times of high rhetorical temperature can create a strange evidence problem:

**understatement and hyperbole may both conceal the middle.**

This can happen when:
- ordinary language becomes identity-coded;
- a word/phrase becomes so associated with one group that other speakers avoid it;
- inflammatory descriptions cause members of a group to stop speaking honestly in mixed spaces;
- contempt, fear or anticipated punishment changes what people are willing to report;
- platform/moderation/access barriers make some ordinary conversation hard to observe;
- different communities use incompatible terms for materially similar needs.

The result is **discursive inaccessibility**: the available public language may not represent the full distribution of private/ordinary experience.

## Neutrality rule

Observe this mechanism symmetrically across political, national, religious, economic and cultural groups.

Do not infer what a person believes from group membership.

Do not classify a population as dangerous, dishonest or irrational from rhetoric about it.

Examples such as hostile treatment of MAGA voters, anti-MAGA voters, migrants, officials, activists, rural residents, wealthy people, unhoused people, religious communities, etc. are investigation prompts only when specific evidence supports the treatment pattern being studied.

## Signals

Track separately:
- **accessibility:** can members speak in the observed venue?
- **self-censorship indication:** direct evidence only; do not infer silently;
- **rhetorical temperature:** ordinary / euphemistic / intensified / dehumanizing etc., with quoted/source-backed examples;
- **semantic poisoning:** formerly broad word becomes strongly group-coded;
- **translation parallax:** same institution shifts tone/agency across language surfaces;
- **treatment asymmetry:** comparable conduct described differently across groups;
- **missing middle:** available discourse clusters at extremes with little observable ordinary language.

Avoid one global score.

## Governor question

When the public conversation is too rhetorically hot or inaccessible, ask:

> **What ordinary experience might be missing from our observable sample, and what lawful, nonintrusive source could make it easier to hear without presuming its content?**

This is a source-coverage question, not permission to fabricate "silent majority" views.

## Connection to pressure points

Language mismatch can hide material stress just as geographic averaging can.

Two groups may argue in incompatible vocabularies while depending on the same:
- job;
- shipment;
- clinic;
- water source;
- school;
- grid;
- road;
- crop;
- social trust function.

The Pressure-Web should look underneath rhetoric for shared dependency while preserving genuine value disagreement.
