# English Parallax Feed ↔ Governor

## Why English gets a dedicated human-facing lane

Jared can evaluate English nuance most directly. Keep the fully granular multilingual system, but add an **English-centered parallax feed** for human interpretation.

This does not make English the authoritative language. It makes English the most useful **human comparison pivot** for Jared.

## Feed in

For each matched official item:

`Chinese original -> official English -> each other official language`

Leaf Bees remain independently read first.

Then construct:
- English ↔ Chinese;
- English ↔ French;
- English ↔ Russian;
- English ↔ Spanish;
- English ↔ Arabic;
- English ↔ any other verified official surface.

Only high-interest items need full foreign↔foreign pairwise comparison.

## Feed out to Governor

Each candidate difference sends:

- English wording;
- counterpart semantic unit, preferably in original script;
- literal/gloss translation if needed;
- difference type;
- routine-translation explanation;
- possible rhetorical/meaning significance;
- historical self-comparison;
- confidence;
- Sample recommendation;
- Question recommendation;
- **ask-Jared** flag.

The Governor may:
- mark routine;
- request another language/control;
- send to Jared as a Sample;
- open/relate a Question;
- ask a Leaf Bee for diachronic comparison;
- defer.

## Feed back from Jared

Jared may respond:
- `routine_to_me`
- `interesting_word_choice`
- `tone_shift`
- `agency_shift`
- `metaphor_shift`
- `I_need_context`
- `show_me_more_like_this`
- freeform note

These are human interpretation signals, not factual verdicts.

The Parallax Bee can use them to refine what deserves future sampling without turning one human's taste into hidden evidence.

## Boss packet

A good batch-level summary can say:

> "38 differences survived matching. 35 look routine after the Noise Gate. Here are the 3 I cannot comfortably dismiss, plus 1 boring control."

That is a **Boss Packet** suitable for Meaning Tower.

The boss is not "beat the foreign language." The boss is:
> **Can we tell signal from translation/weather/noise without flattening meaning?**
