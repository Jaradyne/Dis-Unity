# Regional AI Lab Template — inspired by GIA-UH

## Why GIA-UH is useful as a template

The University of Havana's Artificial Intelligence Group is unusually useful for Dis-Unity because it is not "one chatbot project."

Its official site describes work across:
- natural-language generation/processing/analysis;
- machine learning;
- metaheuristics for complex problems;
- robotics.

Highlighted projects include:
- **Cecilia** — Cuban-language/cultural-context model family;
- **AutoGOAL** — automated program/ML pipeline synthesis;
- **LETO** — LLM-supported knowledge discovery/data analysis;
- **ARGO** — agent reasoning/governance/orchestration framework.

ARGO itself exposes code-first and declarative/YAML agent workflows, typed message-board multi-agent coordination, tools/skills, and an experimental training mode based on collected execution examples and user feedback.

That combination makes the lab a useful **regional ecology template**, not because every region should copy Cuba, but because it shows several complementary organs in one academic group:
- local-language/local-culture modeling;
- orchestration;
- knowledge discovery;
- automated methods;
- open repositories;
- human feedback/training.

## Template for finding analogous groups

For each university/research community, ask:

- What local-language/cultural model work exists?
- What orchestration/agent work exists?
- What public-interest or applied domain work exists?
- What tooling/data infrastructure is open?
- What can run under constrained compute/connectivity?
- What projects invite outside contributions?
- What governance/safety thinking is explicit?
- What local conditions make their designs different from U.S. assumptions?
- Who publishes code/papers/contact channels?
- What could Dis-Unity learn without demanding partnership?

## Candidate archetypes

Look for labs in:
- Latin America/Caribbean;
- Africa;
- South/Southeast Asia;
- East Asia;
- Europe;
- Middle East;
- Pacific;
- Indigenous-language/community research networks.

Do not force one lab per nation. Technical/cultural regions cross borders.

## Output

A Regional Lab Sample should show:
- group;
- region;
- public projects;
- local-context contribution;
- agent/tooling contribution;
- constraints/adaptations;
- open-source/public interfaces;
- why this is interesting to the web;
- no claim of endorsement/partnership.
