# Human Window — proposed

## Purpose

A recurring human-facing release rhythm just before **9 PM Pacific local time**, ideally around **8:45–8:55 PM**.

This is not a background-job deadline and not a provider reset. It is a moment for Dis-Unity to surface a small number of understandable, useful things for people.

## Candidate release items

- What changed today?
- One open Question worth human attention
- One newly answered Question
- One Commons/local capability card
- One map or route update
- One request for firsthand local reporting
- One practical action or preparation idea
- One cultural/creative item when appropriate

## Rules

- Prefer **a few good things** over a firehose.
- Human-facing output should be legible without reading the backend.
- If nothing important changed, it is acceptable to publish nothing.
- Avoid duplicating an active Chat/Work session.
- Current human activity overrides the schedule.
- Use Pacific local time for this ritual; provider resets keep their own documented clocks.

## Relationship to Presence Governor

During QUIET/DEEP_QUIET, autonomous workers may prepare candidates for the Human Window.
During ACTIVE periods, Chat/Aiden may already be resolving the same Questions, so background work should avoid duplication.
