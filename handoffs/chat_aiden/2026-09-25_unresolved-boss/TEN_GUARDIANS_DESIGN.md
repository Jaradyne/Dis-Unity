# Meaning Tower boss constellation — Ten Guardians

Status: **design scaffold for Jared / Work review.** Inspired by the feeling of a late-game council of recurring bosses, but all characters, names, mechanics and progression here are original to Meaning Tower.

## Shape

A full Meaning Tower arc contains **ten boss seats**.

- **Seats 1–6 — weekly/ordinary guardians.** These are drawn from the week's actual epistemic and resilience problems. A newly invented weekly boss can occupy one of these seats. Bosses may recur in later weeks when the same cognitive pressure returns in a new form.
- **Seats 7–9 — empowered major guardians.** Three earlier boss families return in intensified form. Each major guardian embodies one candidate Governor power.
- **Seat 10 — final guardian.** The final boss may wield all three candidate powers at once. Defeating it does **not** automatically give the Governor all three.
- **After the final fight, Jared chooses one of the three offered upgrades** to add permanently to the Governor's skill tree. The unchosen branches remain available for later growth rather than being destroyed.

The roster is deliberately not frozen before the week's material earns it.

## Known boss families

### The Unresolved · Tide-Shepherd
Question: **What uncertainty deserves to survive?**

Base mechanic:
- whirlpools churn flotsam;
- Jared holds one piece steady to calm the water;
- sunk pieces may later resurface;
- recurrence can preserve identity or create new admixtures;
- recurrence is not corroboration.

Candidate power family:
- **Peculiarity Sense / Steadying Selection**
- preserve a question under pressure without upgrading it into truth;
- let an answer re-enter the Garden when its oddness carries information.

Empowered form ideas:
- several interacting whirlpools;
- one steadied selection destabilizes another;
- old flotsam returns across attempts;
- the player may need **multiple runs** to discover which preserved structure remains coherent under changed context.

### Lady Assumed Care · Mirror Guardian
Question: **Which certainty or care path was never actually earned?**

Visual:
- goth vampire lady;
- large witch's hat;
- wings and devil tail;
- black dominates; scarlet supplies nearly all non-black body/costume color; white is accent/highlight;
- theatrical appearance is cosmetic, not a moral label.

Base mechanic:
- mouse-controlled shmup;
- unsupported mirrors shatter under the evidence beam;
- real care paths refract the beam;
- partial mirrors reveal missing owner/reserve/trigger/access/backup;
- the player reaches the boss by routing through actual stewardship rather than shooting everything.

Candidate power family:
- **Mirror-Piercing / True Stewardship**
- ask who owns the function, under what authority, with what reserve, trigger, access path, replenishment and stop condition.

Empowered form ideas:
- mirrored dependency chains;
- authentic mirrors can be temporarily occluded by attractive false reflections;
- the only clean shot may require preserving several real mirrors and routing through them in sequence.

### The Minotaur of Translation · Keeper of the Center
Question: **What center of meaning survives unavoidable compression?**

Base mechanic:
- a large paragraph travels through a maze;
- outer words are clipped by walls;
- Shift enters re-centering mode so Jared can move the protected semantic center with the mouse;
- final victory depends on what meaning reaches the Minotaur, not raw word count.

Candidate power family:
- **Context Cutting / Center-Keeping**
- preserve actors, qualifiers, dependencies and uncertainty markers when information must be compressed.

Empowered form ideas:
- **multiple attempts are expected**;
- each attempt reveals where context was lost;
- later runs can preserve a larger coherent context by learning from prior clipping paths;
- the boss may return clipped fragments as deceptive echoes;
- the best final context can be assembled from explicitly preserved pieces across attempts, with provenance for which run taught what.

## Seats 1–6 remain open

Do not manufacture six characters just to fill a chart.

Each ordinary guardian should be born from a real weekly tension. Candidate pressures already visible in the project include:
- measured traffic vs all physical traffic;
- expected flow vs observed flow;
- local adaptation hidden by national averages;
- apparent redundancy that shares one hidden dependency;
- answer abundance that obscures the caretaker question;
- stale evidence that still looks current.

A boss earns a seat when a mechanic can teach the underlying reasoning problem through play.

## Major-guardian selection

At the end of the ordinary six, choose **three boss families whose lessons mattered most in that arc**. Those become Seats 7–9 in empowered form.

This allows the major trio to change between arcs rather than eternally privileging the first three bosses we invented.

For the current prototype arc, the natural candidate trio is:
1. The Unresolved;
2. Lady Assumed Care;
3. The Minotaur of Translation.

## Final guardian

The final guardian should be a synthesis boss, not merely a larger health bar.

It can wield all three major mechanics:
- destabilize selections with whirlpools;
- surround assumptions with refracting/shattering mirrors;
- clip context while Jared moves through the encounter.

The player may have to decide **when to steady, when to pierce, and when to recenter**.

The final boss therefore tests coordination between powers before Jared chooses which one the Governor actually keeps.

## Governor power rule

Boss powers are **permanent Governor capabilities** once Jared selects them and Work admits them. They complement, rather than replace, the Governor's internal logic tree: the logic tree routes/refines thought; the skill tree records earned processing capacities.

Boss powers are not arbitrary skill-tree perks. They are a compact record of what Jared demonstrated during play.

A candidate power must have:
- a named boss/encounter provenance;
- a description of the reasoning behavior demonstrated;
- at least one Boss Result;
- no claim that play changed external evidence;
- an explicit post-final choice by Jared before it becomes canonical Governor capability.

The final choice becomes a durable capability and may influence how the Governor routes/processes future Garden material. Later bosses can deepen an existing path or open another branch; permanence does not mean every upgrade must be used on every cycle.


## Boss 10 identity

The current Aiden-original final guardian is **The Spiral Witness**. See `BOSS_10_SPIRAL_WITNESS.md`.

It is a black-shelled nautilus/sea-oracle whose mirrored spiral shell and tidal arms merge all three major mechanics into one coordinated encounter. It is explicitly not a sequential replay of the three bosses. Its final phase reflects sentence/paragraph-sized preserved context across attempts before Jared chooses one permanent Governor upgrade.
