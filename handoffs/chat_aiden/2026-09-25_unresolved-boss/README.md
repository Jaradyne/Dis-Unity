# Meaning Tower — The Unresolved + Parallax Collector Bee

Status: **Chat Aiden staging for Work review.** Nothing in this packet schedules a worker, changes canonical research, or authorizes spending.

## Interactive boss

`UNRESOLVED_BOSS.html` is a self-contained retro pixel-art encounter for Meaning Tower.

**The Unresolved · Tide-Shepherd** is a recurring sea-sovereign boss: aqua body, crown, tentacle-beard and lower tentacles. Each round summons a whirlpool carrying several pieces of flotsam. Jared clears the whirlpool by **holding one piece steady** long enough for the water to calm.

The mechanic is deliberately not a multiple-choice test:

- the held piece is preserved for attention;
- the other pieces sink from that round but are **not declared false**;
- there is no score;
- each round records what Jared preserved;
- finishing produces a copyable Boss Result;
- the proposed first power-up is **Peculiarity Sense**: an answer may return to the Garden as a question when its oddness is informative;
- the power is still only a proposed/played result until reviewed integration records it.

The HTML is data-driven through `window.MEANING_TOWER_BOSS_PACKET`. The built-in packet is explicitly illustrative. A later real Translation/Parallax packet can replace the demo rounds without changing the fight mechanics.

The visual uses original canvas/CSS pixel art and no external game assets.

## Bee

`agents/parallax_collector_bee.json` stages the **Parallax Collector Bee** as an available, not-running role.

Its bounded job is:

1. start from one existing Question/epoch and one matched official multilingual source family;
2. preserve both originals and source dates;
3. use the initiating Garden grammar;
4. apply ordinary translation/weather controls before escalating;
5. maintain CROSS attachments as inherited / independent / bridge;
6. retain at most three surviving residuals plus one boring control;
7. emit a Boss Packet for Meaning Tower or exit with a question/silence;
8. never score cultures, infer motive from wording, or auto-spawn another worker.

The proposed Garden destination is `PYGENT:PARALLAX-0001`, but it remains non-runnable until Work deliberately registers a corresponding tree/node.

## What this does not do

- It does not perform the real matched-document research yet.
- It does not award a canonical Governor power automatically.
- It does not instantiate the autonomous Governor or Expected Flow Engine.
- It does not schedule the Bee.
- It does not alter OpenRouter, Cloudflare, Gemini or Mistral provider configuration.
