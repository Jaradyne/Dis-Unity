## Clonal teleport

- **MOVE**: branch leaves the current tree.
- **CLONE**: current branch remains and one linked child enters another tree.
- **FANOUT**: bounded linked children enter several warranted trees.

Example: Economics may continue while cloning a production question and a diplomacy question.

Each child inherits parent pass/Question/epoch, evidence/source refs, the residual/peculiarity that caused the branch, and route authority. Sibling clones are not independent evidence.
