# Institutional Constraints Bee — design note

## Why this is a separate Bee

The logistics system asks whether material functions can keep working through stress. This Bee asks a different but equally inspectable question:

> **Where is governmental power moving, what check used to constrain it, what check remains, and what observable evidence would distinguish ordinary reform from durable concentration?**

It must **not** begin from "autocracy is coming" or "this administration intends collapse." Those are hypotheses, not premises. Deliberate degradation, ordinary reform, wartime/readiness adaptation, legal disagreement, bureaucratic conflict and incompetence all remain competing explanations until evidence separates them.

The Bee is designed to notice mechanisms associated in the comparative-democracy literature with executive aggrandizement while also retaining reversals, judicial/legislative/state resistance, professional noncompliance with improper orders, electoral competition and other countervailing evidence.

## Why V-Dem is a good starting frame but not the sensor

V-Dem supplies a comparative vocabulary and long-run expert-coded indicators. Bright Line Watch provides a faster survey pulse. Freedom House supplies another annual cross-domain external assessment.

None of those should be treated as the project's event ledger or ground truth. The Bee should ingest their releases as **external lenses** and then ground consequential events in primary/public records where possible.

The actual fast layer should watch:
- Federal Register / executive orders / presidential memoranda;
- agency directives and organizational charts;
- OPM, OMB, DOJ, DoD/Service, DHS, intelligence-community public releases;
- GAO, CRS, CIGIE/individual IGs, OSC, OGE and congressional oversight;
- court dockets/opinions/orders;
- Senate confirmations and statutory changes;
- EAC/CISA and state/local election-administration records;
- public personnel announcements and official biographies.

AP/Reuters and domain journalism are discovery/cross-check sources, not automatic substitutes for the underlying record.

## Parent lanes

### A. Oversight & anti-corruption
Inspectors general, ethics, whistleblower protection, GAO access, reporting requirements, public datasets and congressional oversight.

### B. Justice & legal independence
DOJ/FBI/prosecutorial personnel, directives, case interventions, compliance with orders, use of legal/contract/security powers against institutional actors.

### C. Civil service / science / regulation / intelligence
Career protections, reclassification, mass removal/reassignment, advisory bodies, scientific-integrity rules, enforcement independence and intelligence leadership.

### D. Elections
Who controls voter rolls, certification, voting-system custody, election policing, federal observation, ballot access, prosecutions involving election officials, record seizure and displacement of state/local administrators.

### E. Military professionalism
Senior-command turnover, promotion/selection rules, domestic deployments, command chains, legal-review capacity, ORA/spiritual-fitness implementation and any public evidence of assignment/deployment selection effects.

### F. Federalism & civil society
Federal displacement of state/local authority and governmental pressure on universities, law firms, media, nonprofits, contractors or professional associations.

### G. Doctrine & norm genealogy
Follow legal/administrative propositions through advocacy → briefs/memos → opinions/orders → implementation. Similar language is not itself proof of coordination.

### H. Personnel / organizational networks
Map **publicly documented professional and organizational affiliations** among senior officials and projects when consequential. Do not infer religion, ideology or secret coordination.

## Important military caution

Raw evidence that the military is storing more fuel, medicine, munitions or generating independent power is weak evidence of political consolidation on its own. FORTRESS America explicitly describes those as resilience/readiness goals.

The more discriminating questions are:
- who has authority over the new capacity;
- whether appropriations/oversight rules change;
- whether the same capacity is connected to domestic political missions;
- whether personnel or command-selection rules change around it;
- whether public criteria become loyalty/faith/ideology-linked;
- whether ordinary professional or legal checks are displaced.

## ORA high-resolution lane

The new Office of Religious Affairs deserves fine-grained monitoring because its announced remit includes military-wide religious support and, according to the publicly described memo, spiritual-fitness research/assessment.

But **spiritual fitness is not new**. Army doctrine and a 2025 Army guide predate ORA. The correct question is what ORA changes in authority, definitions, data, evaluation, funding and consequences.

The Bee should seek the primary memo and later implementation documents, then track:
- voluntary vs mandatory participation;
- data fields and privacy;
- command access;
- evaluation/promotion/assignment/deployment consequences;
- treatment of nonreligious and minority-faith personnel;
- appeal/accommodation;
- contracts/endorsing organizations;
- staffing/funding;
- complaints/court/IG/FOIA findings;
- changes or reversals.

The Pentagon has publicly stated ORA will not compel participation or favor one faith. That statement is an important **control/prediction**: implementation should be checked against it rather than presumed to violate it.

## "Unusually full of affiliates"

For FORTRESS, ORA, Project Meridian or any other initiative, "many affiliates" cannot be evaluated without defining:
1. what counts as an affiliation;
2. which roles are in the denominator;
3. the comparison set;
4. the expertise that would ordinarily produce overlap.

Allowed edges: public employment, formal advisory/board roles, authorship/signature of policy work, publicly documented organizational office. Do not infer private belief from school, church proximity, social media follows, family or geography.

The output should be a network with provenance, **not an accusation**.

## Retroactive baseline

Start current, then backfill only what improves discrimination.

Good baseline windows:
- same office/function under prior administrations;
- previous wartime/emergency episodes;
- normal turnover for comparable positions;
- earlier election cycles;
- pre- and post-policy doctrine.

Avoid a giant historical crawl before the current event schema works.

## No overall autocracy score

This Bee should never produce "America is X% authoritarian" or a regime forecast.

It can say:
- a documented check weakened/strengthened;
- authority moved;
- implementation occurred/did not;
- a court/Congress/state body blocked or narrowed it;
- a personnel network exists;
- a claimed pattern lacks a denominator;
- an unresolved mechanism deserves a child.

That is enough for the Garden and Governor to reason from.
