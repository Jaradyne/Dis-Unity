# Commons metabolism and usefulness record

This is a qualitative mapping interface, not a scorecard and not a census.

A commons can be tiny, specialized, mobile, informal, temporary or highly developed. It does not need every capability below.

## Identity and place

- name / local description
- geography and service reach
- indoor / outdoor / mobile / hybrid
- who can alter the space and how
- ordinary access conditions
- barriers to access
- ownership/stewardship/control if publicly known

## Metabolism

For every meaningful capability record:

**needs** — inputs required to function  
**produces** — useful outputs/services/knowledge  
**maintains** — what must be repaired, replenished or cared for  
**can_spare** — surplus available without weakening essential users  
**needs_back** — useful return loads or reciprocal help  
**fails_if** — shared failure conditions  
**fallback** — alternate way to preserve the function  
**survives_unplugged** — what remains useful without grid/network/software/account access  
**activation_time** — how quickly the capability can become useful  
**replenishment_path** — how it gets its next inputs  
**knowledge_resilience** — whether practical knowledge survives one organizer/maintainer being absent  
**modifiability** — whether users can actually change/improve the place  
**beauty_and_belonging** — qualitative signs that the place supports ordinary life, not only emergency survival

## Participation texture — never a hidden headcount

Allowed qualitative language:

- **a few**
- **a small gathering**
- **a steady gathering**
- **many**
- **crowds**
- **crowds upon crowds**

Do not convert these labels into secret numeric ranges. Do not track named attendance histories. The purpose is only to understand whether a capability feels unused, comfortably active, strained, or overwhelmed.

Useful observations include:
- people linger rather than merely transact;
- people return;
- people teach one another;
- people alter or improve the place;
- knowledge survives a particular person's absence;
- demand exceeds comfortable capacity;
- the commons can birth or support another node rather than endlessly centralizing.

## Capability strands

Avoid treating commons as a single maturity ladder. Map parallel strands such as:

- place / comfort / beauty
- tools / repair / making
- learning / documentation
- food / kitchens / gardens / compost / nutrient circulation
- water
- energy / charging / refrigeration / thermal refuge
- biological energy/resource cycling (for example anaerobic digestion/biogas where feedstock, safety, maintenance and permitting fit; algal photobioreactors as research or coproduct/fuel systems rather than assumed bulk power)
- communications
- mobility / logistics
- health-supportive services within appropriate scope
- culture / celebration
- cooperative production / shared economic capability
- reserves / mutual aid / reciprocal routes
- long-horizon experimentation

A commons can be excellent in one strand and absent in another without being “immature.”

## Birth condition

A new commons/node becomes worth investigating when concentration itself is beginning to destroy accessibility, redundancy or local fit, or when a learned capability can be copied elsewhere at modest cost.

That parallels agent birth: **do not average away useful local difference and do not centralize merely because growth is possible.**
