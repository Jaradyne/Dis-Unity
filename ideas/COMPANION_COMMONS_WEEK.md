# A little piece of home

For the September 26–October 3 companion week: a design/evidence notebook, not a booked event or an available partner.

Begin with [Start with a table](../posters/START_WITH_A_TABLE_TRI_FOLD.md) and [commons growth](COMMONS_GROWTH_PATH.md). Leave one understandable starting activity with enough real information that someone can decide whether to try it.

**Working candidate:** a shared making-and-learning table. Someone brings a repair question, a sketch, something they want to learn, or simply their company. Joining and resting do not require a productivity performance. Beauty, conversation and capability can grow together.

| Question | Useful thing to leave behind |
|---|---|
| What would make this feel like somewhere we belong? | A short invitation in Jared's voice, still a draft. |
| What can people do at the first table? | One activity using available resources, with unknowns explicit. |
| Where could it happen and who can reach it? | One public candidate with dated hours, access, permission questions and an alternative. A listing does not establish availability. |
| Who carries the work, including rest and cleanup? | A responsibility map with gaps where nobody has volunteered. |
| What would let it grow without overwhelming someone? | A reusable tool, instruction, skill or reciprocal connection. |

Keep it small: intended experience, actual function, access, resources confirmed versus missing, one next experiment, one reflection. Complete institutional readiness is not a condition for beginning to imagine or participate.

Connect discoveries to existing commons/geography Questions after reading their live IDs/epochs. Keep private participant details out of public Git. Outreach, bookings, spending and promises need their own authorization for a concrete proposal. For this week, research and prepare; never invent a willing host or volunteer.

Carry this question back to the Governor: **What became more possible for someone, and what still stands between the promise and their arrival?**
