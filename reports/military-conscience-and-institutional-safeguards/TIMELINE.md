# Timeline: military institutions, DHS and domestic enforcement, 1945–2026

**92 dated records across the original and expanded lanes.** Some records describe different aspects of the same development; they are not independent-event or confirmation counts. The [combined ledger](expanded_events.json) preserves every original payload and field. The original [RC-005 ledger](events.json) remains unchanged.

This is a selective institutional history. The 1945 anchor includes documents from the closing months of the war as context for the postwar settlement. Earlier developments first collected here are **late discoveries**, not new October events. Publication, observation, enactment, implementation and retrieval dates are kept distinct. FY-only labels and intervals are not converted into invented days. Sources resolve in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).
