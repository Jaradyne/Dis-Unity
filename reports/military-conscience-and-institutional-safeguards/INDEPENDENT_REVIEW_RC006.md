## Challenge to the overall inference

The packet identifies concrete personnel, equipment, contracting and custody connections while leaving the asserted ORA-to-ICE religious-selection link unestablished. That is an appropriate limit on the causal claim, not grounds to dismiss documented coercion or abuse. The reports also avoid treating paper safeguards, institutional separation, returned detainees or judicial victories as proof that effective prevention and remedy were available when needed.

The removal chapter distinguishes investigative findings, litigants' claims and judicial findings. Its accounts of Khalil, Öztürk, Abrego Garcia and the Venezuelan CECOT cohort do not merge different people, jurisdictions or custody histories. This reviewer did not independently repeat every interview-based source or audit each complete court docket. Its findings are therefore accepted at the source-attributed level stated in the chapter, not promoted to independent population-wide verification.
