## Corrections and distinctions retained

- Spiritual fitness, assessment, chapel youth programs and civilian lawyer details have predecessors. Novelty must be assigned to the changed authority, rules, scale or coupling.
- Three service nomination requests do not establish three removals of sitting TJAGs; the Navy office was already vacant.
- A600-lawyer requested ceiling is not simultaneous deployments or a measured share of military legal capacity. Selected EOIR appointment notices cannot be added into a current staffing total.
- Religious preference codes, dog-tag wording and chaplain endorsement eligibility are separate systems.
- The school offering is advertised as elective, and its public rollout predates ORA. Actual classroom practice and practical alternatives remain unknown.
- A policy safeguard is evidence of a formal constraint, not evidence of enforcement. A stated budget deadline is not an appropriation or a public-release promise.
- AP syndications and memo mirrors are shared source families. Multiple articles do not multiply the underlying events.
