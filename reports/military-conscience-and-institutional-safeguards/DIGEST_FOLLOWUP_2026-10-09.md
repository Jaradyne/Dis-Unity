## Iceberg-head / apparently benign signal rule — propose for the Bee

A slogan or policy phrase can carry an ordinary benign use and, in some contexts, signal exclusionary group boundaries. **Do not classify a person, denomination or private belief by a phrase in isolation.** Collect only publicly attributable institutional speech/actions and evaluate context:

- exact wording, speaker's public role, official versus personal capacity, venue, date, audience and full surrounding material;
- ordinary religious/cultural interpretation and historic usage;
- use alongside explicit exclusion, dehumanization, preferential state support, unequal eligibility, personnel selection or noncompliance with conscience protections;
- whether agencies repeat it in operating documents, curricula, contracts, commands or decisions;
- whether its referent shifts across versions (e.g., all personnel → favored constituency);
- counterexamples, corrections, actual remedies and evidence that a suspected link did **not** occur;
- no inference of private religion, xenophobia, motive or coordination from a slogan.

Suggested output is **contextual-review flag**, not guilt score, extremist classification or alarm. It can spawn a narrow historical/implementation Question when supported by an identifiable consequence or materially consequential language shift. Benign religious expression alone is a normal control. "Christ is King" is a sincere theological declaration for many Christians; contextual institutional usage must be evaluated on observed conduct rather than classified by its words alone.

Use **anticipatory obedience / preemptive compliance** as a *mechanism to study*, not a default description of service members. Possible public indicators: changes in complaint reporting/denominators, dated anonymous **aggregate** unit climate surveys, policy acknowledgments, participation/alternative access, assignment/promotion process documents, verifiable contemporaneous public accounts and inspector-general case dispositions. A suppressed complaint rate may mimic an improved climate. No private belief or individual unit dossiers.

## FOIA strategy — request documents, not confidential belief dossiers

The Office of the Secretary of Defense / Joint Staff operates an electronic FOIA portal: https://pal.whs.mil/app/MainPage.aspx . It is **only** for OSD/Joint Staff records; service-specific records must go to the relevant service component. Older archival material may require NARA. The portal allows electronic submission, status tracking and responses.

Draft reviewable, narrow requests **before** any sending:
- 9/30/2026 ORA establishing memo: appointed director, operative charter, delegated authority, assessment/privacy instrument/data dictionary/access controls, issued policies and dated budgets/funding or solicitations; seek nonpersonal administrative records and releasable versions, not soldier answers.
- DoDEA: the exact Western Civilization to 1500 syllabus/course-map, textbook/edition, assigned materials, assessment/grading guidance, elective alternative availability and any documented procurement/license/partnership.
- Marine Corps / relevant personnel components: versioned religious-affiliation category lists, implementation guidance, role-based access policy, purpose limitations, aggregate resource impact, consent/refusal process, change logs and privacy impact assessment.
- JAG/DOJ/OSD: actual monthly counts of military attorney details by component and duty-day totals, backfill/filled-billet data and service demand metrics; avoid inferring all 600 requested slots were used.
- DEOCS/OPA: publicly releasable year-comparable aggregate survey questions/findings on inclusion, retaliation, perceived fairness, command climate and safe complaint paths, with small-cell suppression maintained.

Ask for reasonably segregable nonexempt portions and fee notification/waiver where applicable. FOIA cannot compel agencies to create nonexistent aggregate analyses, and privacy/confidentiality, security and deliberative exemptions may apply. A request is not evidence of wrongdoing. **No request should be filed without Jared's explicit later instruction.**
