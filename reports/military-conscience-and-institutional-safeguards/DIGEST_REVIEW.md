## Six priority collections

1. **ORA charter and appointments.** Compare duties, delegation, review signatures and reporting lines with the September 30 memo and 2024 Board arrangement. Record every version and effective date. Stop after the first operative charter and organization chart, or a documented bounded search failure.
2. **Assessment and data rules.** Obtain the current instrument and instructions, privacy documentation, data dictionary, access/retention rules and refusal policy. Record whether evaluation is individual or aggregate and whether answers reach command. Compare with FM7-22 §10-28. A general spiritual-readiness label is not enough.
3. **Youth and homeschooling implementation.** Identify the first public funding allocation or solicitation under ORA. Compare provider eligibility, learning requirements, opt-in consent, equivalent alternatives and complaints. Existing chapel programs are the baseline, not automatically new events.
4. **One course audit.** Obtain the Western Civilization syllabus, exact textbook edition, assigned readings and grading rubric, plus the alternative world-history offering. Evaluate representative disputed passages in context. Keep curriculum governance separate from ORA until a record connects them.
5. **Legal capacity before and after.** Use an actual service implementation order and matched staffing/workload dates. Reconcile detailees, renewals, reimbursement and backfill. The 600 ceiling and selected appointment notices cannot substitute for deployed lawyer-days or lost capacity.
6. **Remedy effectiveness.** Follow one public, documented complaint or oversight action from filing through disposition. Record what was alleged, what was found, remedy, appeal and reversal. Do not infer prevalence from advocacy case intake without denominators.

## What would strengthen or weaken the concern?

**Strengthen:** compulsory profession or participation; materially inferior alternatives; belief or spiritual-score fields reaching selection boards; documented retaliation; contracts favoring one belief without a support-need justification; transfers that disable independent review; repeated substantiated cases across distinct commands.

**Weaken or narrow:** usable opt-outs without consequences; equivalent access across beliefs and nonbelief; individual data barred from career systems; independent complaints upheld and remedied; transparent need-based allocations; actual backfill preserving legal service; correction of exclusionary rules. These observations would constrain particular mechanisms, not necessarily settle the entire institutional picture.

Compare like populations and preserve time at risk, location, command, rank, occupational field, eligibility and available alternatives. Publicly stated institutional affiliations can be relevant; names, schools, family ties or social-media follows do not establish a person's private belief or motive. No individual belief dossiers are needed.

**Evidence available before a tipping point:** appropriations and obligations, command delegations, staff qualification rules, contracts, assessment data fields, permitted force, inspection findings, complaint access, remedy delays and published court-compliance records. None requires public spiritual scores for individual soldiers. Missing records constrain certainty; they do not require waiting for catastrophic harm before recognizing institutional risk.

**Strengthening observations:** capacity and discretion grow together; targeting expands into protected expression; force rules broaden to passive conduct; transfers obstruct effective review; repeated deficiencies persist after notice; oversight loses staff or access without an effective substitute. **Weakening or narrowing observations:** timely independent review halts improper action, erroneous classifications are corrected, meaningful alternatives work, force limits are enforced and public records demonstrate durable compliance. Test each mechanism rather than assigning a composite danger score.

For every serious stress, name a usable buffer: legal or medical access, safe suspension or release, alternative reception, transparent staffing and records, independent review, outside support and a practical correction route. Record whether each is merely authorized or demonstrably available. Unknown community, legal-aid and receiving-country capacity stays unknown.
