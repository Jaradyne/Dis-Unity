## Digestion diagnosis

This is **a continuity-and-coupling threshold**, not an evidence threshold at which a single overarching allegation becomes established.

Work's most important success is retroactive separation of four kinds of material:
- longstanding religious support, chaplaincy, fitness and civilian-lawyer detail practice;

- legal, practical and independent counterchecks, including interruptions and additions.

Jared's concern can be tested as a **possible institutional mechanism**:

```text
A. authority / resources reorganized
               |
B. formation, assessment, participation or affiliation made visible
               |
C. visibility enters selection, reward, assignment or sanction decisions
               |
D. practical ability to refuse, appeal or challenge becomes weaker
               |
E. disparate careers, access or remedies appear in comparable populations
```

These are **hypothesized edges**, not a causal chain that the current packet establishes. A senior office sharing responsibility for policy and assessment establishes some organizational capacity (A), not that personal religious information is available to a promotion board (B→C), that it is used for personnel decisions (C), or that the legal checks fail (D). The fact that older programs exist is not evidence that changed scale, coupling, data flows or command authority cannot matter. Conversely, overlapping dates or leadership do not establish a single program.

The principal review question is best framed as:

> **Can service members of any faith or none participate or decline without a career penalty, retain equivalent access to support, and obtain usable independent remedies when those protections are breached?**

The broader connected question is what institutional changes could make this protection more or less effective. This avoids reducing the whole investigation either to rhetoric or to the absence of an already documented loyalty test.

## Six knots to retain

### 1. Authority is not implementation — but authority is not nothing

The September 30 ORA memorandum creates a direct-report policy/resource/assessment office, while explicitly protecting freedom of conscience and forbidding compelled religious participation (O01). Both clauses are operative research objects. Verify the future implementing charter and office/board reporting line, then test what happened under each authority rather than assuming the disclaimer either settles or nullifies concerns.

### 2. Assessment is a **data-path problem**

FM 7-22's self-selected/self-assessed, no-repercussion protection (B01) sits alongside the February 2025 Army article's language anticipating team/squad leader spiritual assessments (B05). A program proposal is not a current mandatory evaluation. The serious question is who can observe an individual's answers and whether any recorded dimension crosses into a decision-making system.

A useful architecture for collection is:
`instrument -> consent/refusal -> personally identifying fields -> storage -> access -> aggregation -> commander visibility -> evaluation/assignment decision -> remedy`.

Keep confidential chaplain communications, voluntary self-assessments, aggregated unit trends and individually identifiable command assessments rigorously separate.

### 3. Youth formation, DoDEA school curriculum, and accession are distinct lanes

ORA's resource remit explicitly includes chapel youth programs and faith-based homeschooling support (O01); earlier chapel youth support existed (O06). Separately, a DoDEA elective using *The Golden Thread* is documented for 2026–27 (O04/O05), and its rollout predates ORA. No examined document demonstrates curricular control by ORA or a child's school participation affecting service-member promotion or later accession. Follow contracts/choice/alternatives, classroom assessment and actual accession rules **without inventing a bridge between these institutions**.

### 4. Legal independence has formal constraints **and** operational capacity

JAG/OGC reshaping, temporary details and Military Justice Review Panel interruption warrant scrutiny (J09–J17). Preexisting statutory duties and the new FY2026 NDAA notification/advice requirements (J03) are concrete counterchecks. A written legal protection is neither proof it worked nor mere decoration. Track actual lawyer-days, reporting pathways, backfill, complaint outcomes and independence of review. Separate service nominations, acting offices, removals, appointment flow, deployed detail headcount and funded billets.

### 5. A cumulative mechanism needs linked **edges**, not piled-up events

Work's `events.json` preserves dates, source families, stage and controls. It does not yet explicitly represent whether a link *between* events is observed, merely proposed, contradicted or rejected. Add a future optional **relationship packet**:
- `from_event_id`, `to_event_id`, relation kind (`precedes`, `same_authority`, `implements`, `transfers_resource`, `feeds_data`, `affects_decision`, `displaces_remedy`, `contradicts`, `counteracts`);
- `relationship_state`: `hypothesized`, `documented`, `partly_documented`, `contested`, `refuted`, `unknown`;
- exact positive, negative and ordinary-comparator source references;
- evidence lineage, domain/time scope, date lag, causal alternatives;
- one discriminating next record and an explicit rest/expiry condition.

**Temporal adjacency should never silently become a factual edge.** Keep ordinary `precedes` and `same_leader` distinct from documented `caused` or `implements`. A relationship object can survive for later review without asserting coordination.

### 6. Counterchecks must be measured at the same level as the concern

Do not only collect an announced policy and ask if an enacted counter-policy exists. Match the layers:
- policy vs policy;
- data-system visibility vs privacy/access enforcement;
- personnel action vs comparable outcomes and actual remedy;
- legal staff diversion vs filled billets, duty days and service demand;
- school advertising vs actual syllabus, alternatives and grading;
- formal complaint rights vs filings, timeliness, dispositions and appeals.

A successful appeal is meaningful evidence of a working remedy, with a limited denominator; it is not proof all commands are safe. A reported complaint likewise does not establish prevalence without exposed population and opportunity to observe.
