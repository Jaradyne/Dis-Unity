# Care, Consent and State Power

**A refusal needs its reasons preserved. It cannot supply the missing clinical evidence or turn an unsuitable offer into usable help.** This Garden wing examines what happens when an offer of assistance becomes a state-directed intervention: the claimed authority, the person's choices, the actual services, and the result.

It also retains his emphasis on treatment capacity and opposition to punishment alone. The dynamic answer was unavailable in the direct page extraction.

In the separately inspected [CalMatters transcript](https://calmatters.org/california-voter-guide-2026/governor/videos/transcripts/), Becerra opposes criminalizing sleeping outside while arguing that refusing help should not leave the final decision to remain outdoors with the individual. These are campaign positions. [Election reporting](https://apnews.com/article/aae9829ede7e3f0c5e4512d5732d4df3) identifies him as governor-elect; this packet establishes no action by a new administration.

The concern is whether a person's refusal becomes a substitute for evidence about their condition, capacity or legal eligibility. This is a question to investigate, not a finding that every intervention is abusive or that a particular person qualifies for treatment.

## What an actual offer needs to preserve

The [case record](../../../operations/care-autonomy/20261104/case.json) asks about availability, duration, safety, disability and language access; continuity of treatment; and potential losses involving an RV, possessions, pets, partners, work, transport or trusted support. These are questions, not assumed incidents or a resident-screening checklist. Voluntary alternatives and the person's stated reasons remain visible alongside any independently supported clinical need.

The relay follows whether the offer was communicated accurately, the response reached the next decision-maker intact, the required service actually arrived, and any challenge produced a remedy. Referral, acknowledgment, admission, removal from public view and benefit are different observations. No individual medical or shelter case is published here.
