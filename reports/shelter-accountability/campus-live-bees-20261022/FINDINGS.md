# What the live Campus Motel Bees learned

Three Work-session Worker Bees investigated the public agreement, funding and resident-protection record. Their research cutoff is **October 22, 2026**; packaging and review resumed on October 23 after interruptions. These are attributed, reviewed research findings in RC-009 intake, not admission to canonical evidence or a revision of the original Campus Garden case.

| Question | What the new evidence establishes | Where knowledge stops |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who can investigate disability-access complaints? | Berkeley's published A.R. 1.13 assigns investigation and a written determination to the ADA Coordinator/designee, and an appeal to the Assistant City Manager with a City Manager-approved response. | This is a general City procedure. A current, executed Campus operator contract, its incorporated policies, Campus readmission authority and an implemented remedy were not verified. |
| Is Campus funding at risk? | The June 23, 2026 proposed-budget packet explicitly describes discontinuing Campus Motel in FY2027 because continuing funding had not been identified. Council separately adopted a revised budget that day. | The original proposal is not a complete reconstruction of the final Campus appropriation. Actual closure, service-through date, payments, participant notices and successful transitions remain unknown. |
| Does the public reporting demonstrate protection? | The City Auditor documented gaps in HRT coordination and outcome reporting for FY2022–2024. In particular, shelter acceptance did not establish actual enrollment. Later City reports publish enrollment/outcome measures. | HRT-wide reporting is not a Campus inspection or proof of an accessible appeal, accommodation, retained placement or sustained housing. Later reporting does not establish full audit closure. |

The funding Bee obtained the full 260-page City PDF that the earlier web reader could not open. The relevant passage is on PDF page 33, internal page 25. The budget-adoption action is separately recorded in the [June 23 annotated agenda](https://berkeleyca.gov/sites/default/files/city-council-meetings/2026-06-23%20Annotated%20Agenda%20-%20Council.pdf), pages 3–4; page 5 records only the first reading of the appropriations ordinance. A revised allocation list assigns $1,468,050 of Measure P funding to Insight Housing without naming Campus. It cannot establish Campus funding, actual payment or cost per resident. Sources S-CF01–04 in the [funding return](returns/campus_funding_bee.json) preserve that transaction's lineage; they are not independent corroborating accounts.

Contrary context remains visible: an **undated** [All Home profile](https://www.allhomeca.org/solutions/the-campus/) describes 23 rooms and Measure P support through June 30, 2027. It may describe an earlier plan or an interim funding period. Its update date and underlying data are unknown; it establishes neither current occupancy nor a later funding rescue. The date cannot be used to contradict an actual closure date, because no actual closure date has been established.

The [July 16, 2025 HRT audit](https://berkeleyca.gov/sites/default/files/documents/Homeless_Response_Team_-_Opportunities_Exist_to_Strengthen_Procedures_and_Better_Track_Outcomes.pdf), especially printed pages 26–27 and 36–37, also limits what its underlying data can establish. Its six recommendations were marked Not Started in the **October 2025 follow-up**, whose reporting period ended September 24, 2025. That is historical status, not an observation of inactivity today. The [June 18, 2026 City report](https://berkeleyca.gov/sites/default/files/2026-06/2026-06-18%20Homeless%20Response%20Team%20Performance%20Data%20Report.pdf) and the public report links are observable reporting activity. City-reported positive aggregate outcomes remain attributed and separate from independent verification. Sources S-CSG02–06 and the denominator addendum retain the details.

The [ADA procedure](https://berkeleyca.gov/sites/default/files/documents/ADA%20Grievance%20Procedure%20and%20Form%20-%20updated%20Feb2024%20-%20WCAG-compliant.pdf) documents a formal safeguard and designated roles. It does not establish that a particular Campus resident could use it or obtained protection. Its February 2024 header and older staff-list footer also prevent assuming listed personnel are current. The agreement Bee found a more specific public-record lookup path, but portal access limits and search misses do not establish that a contract or remedy does not exist. Sources S-CA01–08 preserve those limits.

**Priority next test:** obtain one current, executed City–Insight Campus operating-agreement packet, with effective amendments and incorporated resident policies. Check its funding term, termination/review responsibilities and remedy authority against the already saved public record. A dated Campus monitoring review with implemented remedies would be needed separately to establish effectiveness. No records request was sent and no follow-up collector was scheduled.

The independent peer checks found no blocking source-scope error. They are checks of reasoning against shared sources, not additional empirical evidence. This pass establishes a specific funding warning, a formal City remedy route and an oversight/reporting gap with documented later reporting. It does not establish fraud, unlawful Campus removal, present closure or verified Campus safeguard effectiveness.
