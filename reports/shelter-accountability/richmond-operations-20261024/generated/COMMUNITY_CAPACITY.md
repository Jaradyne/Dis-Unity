## Contrary evidence and ordinary explanations

- The City proposal included capital reimbursement and accepted in-kind matching support; routing through an operator does not by itself establish that builders were denied all funding or that money was retained improperly.
- Hope was historically appointed, and opening publicity may repeat that identity. Such statements cannot settle the user-reported later operator change or current cost barrier.
- A smaller mixed-period historical budget neither disproves a higher current proposal nor verifies it. Terms and scope must match before comparing.
- Developer/press opening statements are retained as contrary context; variable or stale page wording and repeated Hope identification do not authenticate a current operator or occupancy.
- RT-TERM-CONFLICT’s earlier Section 1.4 locator is superseded by Section 1.2, PDF p.3. The retained report/attachment term discrepancy remains unresolved.
- Specialist services, supervision, insurance and fixed staffing costs may matter even at small sites. Whether each current requirement is necessary or proportionate needs its actual written basis and priced scope.
- The operator may have been used as the accountable grantee for reporting and reimbursement. This is a possible explanation; the record does not establish why the recipient changed or whether TVS could meet the conditions.
- Community management with separately purchased specialist support, a shared administrator, or an amended grantee arrangement are alternatives to test, not authorized or funded replacements.

## Where knowledge stops

- No current replacement-operator priced proposal, signed replacement agreement, actual staff roster or dated occupancy record was obtained.
- The roughly $1 million annual demand and resulting blockage remain a user-reported lead; neither verified nor disproved by the older budget.
- The precise City staff decision and legal basis requiring an operational service provider are not identified in the inspected report; community stewardship eligibility was not resolved.
- Actual City disbursements to Hope, pass-through to TVS, unpaid builder costs, unspent balances and a transfer/re-award decision remain unverified.
- Older operator appointments and opening announcements do not establish the current operator or usable capacity. Missing replacement records do not establish absence.
- Formal safeguards, proposed service staffing and an insurance statement do not establish actual resident protection or successful admission.
- The 2026 staff report and attachment disagree on grant term. The attachment retains a mixed-period 2025 startup budget; no annualization or current per-bed rate is derived.
