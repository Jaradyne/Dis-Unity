# RC-001 operational record and limits

## Work actually performed

The coordinator created COMMON_STATE.md and launched six concurrent specialists. Each received the source/date/epistemic protocol and a distinct primary assignment. All six returned substantive findings via messages. The coordinator published a checkpoint and sent specific deepening instructions: energy and food on diesel/harvest and sulfur/phosphate; local on temporary funding and exhausted transport substitutes; skeptic on all three strongest clusters.

A shared usage limit ended all six specialist turns before final file submissions or a full closing review. No completed specialist reports exist. The inbox JSON files are explicitly labeled coordinator captures of the messages that were received. The coordinator reopened the key primary records, examined counterevidence, added public local resources, and built the canonical state. This record prevents a future cycle from mistaking interrupted agent work for completed coverage.

## Independent evidence versus repeated reporting

- Diesel retail prices and weekly stocks are different EIA measurements, but the below-100m forecast and observed stock are not independent stress events.
- USDA sector forecasts, company financial reports and harvest interviews inform different parts of a mechanism. Their shared exposure to fuel is not three separate shocks.
- Nutrien phosphate and potash figures come from one disclosure. They show internal differentiation, not independent confirmation of a global trend.
- BART and SFMTA have separate budget records but correlated regional funding exposure.
- Reuters relaying Bloomberg's unverified Saudi cancellation account is one reporting chain. It remains quarantined from serious warning claims.

## Review outcomes

The ten recorded decisions narrow warnings around observed costs and conditional continuity risks. None establishes imminent systemic failure. Broad claims of industrial collapse, already-realized fuel depletion, universal fertilizer contraction, or current national social escalation were not supported. Regional and annual averages also cannot dismiss a documented local incident.

## Unfinished independent work

The closing skeptic pass must be completed when agent capacity is available. Its first tasks are to reproduce important source comparisons, inspect donor reserve claims and identify any suppressed counterevidence. It should review this state as a critic, not inherit its conclusions as facts.

## Major coverage gaps

No current facility-level water, hospital, fuel-terminal, cold-storage, transformer spare, telecom backup or accessible transport inventory was obtained. No live country-by-country island-of-stability survey was completed. Roads, bridges, insurance, municipal finance beyond the principal transit budgets, small-business exits, agricultural labor/irrigation and processing constraints need targeted follow-up. Current tariff schedules were not comprehensively reconciled; no universal current tariff rate or tariff-caused maintenance failure is asserted. Social coverage has an annual reported-crime baseline and cooperation examples, with current trust/conflict lanes marked unknown.

## Scope boundaries

The project performed public research and created reviewable proposals. It did not contact anyone, commit organizational resources, purchase equipment, enroll in programs, connect private accounts or schedule recurring tasks. An active session can resume the multi-agent workflow from saved state. No background research is running after this cycle.
