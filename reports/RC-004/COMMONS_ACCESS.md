# Commons that can grow — RC-004

Evidence cutoff: 21 September 2026. Bounded research; no outreach or site commitment.

A commons can begin with modifiable space and people making it comfortable and useful. The earlier emphasis on durable agreements was too restrictive as an entry condition. Shade, seating, light, water access, shared tools, small-scale growing and relationships can develop before a full institution or engineered hub. Longer tenure and more infrastructure can protect that work as it grows.

## Access can fail without a building failing

Pandemic closure, restrictive rules, reduced hours and informal exclusion can remove usable refuge capacity even when electricity and the building remain sound. These are conditional mechanisms, not a claim that a specific new municipal law exists. Record who can actually use a node and when, not only its equipment.

A nearby outdoor or encampment-led node could keep relationships, information, supplies and some everyday services operating beyond indoor opening hours. It need not begin as a city-designated hub. Its own limits differ: heat, smoke, flood/rain exposure, water/sanitation, equipment security, replenishment and continuity of access. No particular park’s availability or occupation permission is assumed.

The test is whether the alternative remains usable under the same event that closes the first node. Two nodes sharing one funder, delivery route or exclusionary access rule may provide less independent reserve than their number suggests.
