# What RC-003 contains

RC means Resilience Cascade; 003 is the third committed research cycle. These are cumulative records, not 42 emergencies or 77 independent confirmations.

The 42 signal records consist of 37 FACT, three EARLY SIGNAL and two PLAUSIBLE MECHANISM labels. FACT applies to the bounded observation: for example, the existence of a forecast is factual, while its forecast outcome is not yet observed. Signals include improvements, historical baselines, institutional capacity and limitations.

## All 21 conditional links

These are hypotheses about dependency, including beneficial adaptation. Each carries a mechanism, evidence, activation conditions, buffers, alternatives, horizon, uncertainty and confirmation/falsification observations. A stored link is not an activated failure.

| ID | Dependency | Mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EDGE01 | diesel → trucking | Fuel paid before freight invoices settle can squeeze carrier working capital. |
| EDGE02 | trucking → food_logistics | Higher delivered cost or missed windows can reduce food access and increase spoilage. |
| EDGE03 | diesel → agriculture | Essential field operations have low short-run deferrability and consume working capital. |
| EDGE04 | fertilizer → agriculture | Insufficient timely nutrient access can alter application and future yields; nutrients differ. |
