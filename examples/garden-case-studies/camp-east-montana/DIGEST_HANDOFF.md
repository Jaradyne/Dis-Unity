# Digest handoff: Camp East Montana

**Answer:** Safeguards existed in policy and contract, but the required inspection was skipped before people were housed, multiple detention standards failed in documented practice, and missing contract surveillance machinery weakened enforcement. The reviewed evidence establishes some remedial steps, **not verified resolution of the safety failures**.

The Army awarded the July 18, 2025 WEXMAC task order on ICE's behalf after failed ICE procurement attempts; ICE already led operational oversight. Administration transferred October 1. The authority trail, competing agency explanations, and dated source details are in the [case study](README.md) and [ledger](evidence.json). Parent source C01 and events IC02/IC03 remain unchanged.

**Capacity matters:** approximately 1,600 people at February 2026 end versus 5,000 beds/services paid for. ICE cited isolation and segregation needs; GAO noted subdividable units. DHS later proposed a 4,000-bed tiered model. That proposal is not measured current capacity. [C01 pp2,5,12,27]

**Do not flatten the disagreement:** Defense agreed to lessons work while disputing GAO's balance and conclusions. DHS asked to close the inspection recommendation based on existing policy; GAO pointed to the actual failure to follow it. The February inspection's acceptable rating coexisted with 49 deficiencies and three priority issues. [C01 pp16,22,28–30]

**Later pricing check:** C02, published September 24, states the replacement contract still lacked the cost-saving measures as of August 2026 (pp7–8). This verifies dated nonimplementation of pricing reform, not present safety conditions.

**Open ≠ no corrective work.** All four GAO recommendations were Open on the October 12 recheck. GAO's historical opening-deficiency table expressly excludes subsequent fixes. Contractor-reported work, oversight visits, replacement procurement and Army milestones must remain visible; they also do not prove effective correction. The four recommendation statuses are not a 49-item safety-closure ledger.
