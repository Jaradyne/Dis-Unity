## WHAT MOVED

The strongest new organizing finding is a question: **does the event creating need also weaken the fallback?** This test now applies to every reserve. It joins the conditional replacement protocol, beginning with diesel-to-electric functions.

Food-bank dependence on surplus is substantial but incomplete. Feeding America reports 4.3 billion pounds rescued out of 7.2 billion pounds sourced in FY2025—about 60%, calculated from rounded national figures. ACCFB separately documents purchased food, government supply and joint procurement. These are historical operating evidence, not today's local inventory. The potential squeeze is rising need plus reduced donations plus higher procurement costs; no current famine is established. [Feeding America report](https://www.feedingamerica.org/sites/default/files/2025-12/FA_25AnnReport_DIGITAL_final.pdf), [ACCFB report](https://www.accfb.org/annual-report-2025/).

An unexpected local parallel is usable public space: Hayward reduced library hours effective January 2026 to address its budget deficit. A building can remain while access contracts. No resulting heat injury or outage failure is established. [City notice](https://www.hayward-ca.gov/discover/news/jan26/library-hours-be-reduced-two-digital-streaming-resources-eliminated).

The upstream nutrient warning remains material: Mosaic's August 27 disclosure documents phosphate idles/curtailments attributed to sulfur availability and affordability. The physical production constraint is stronger evidence than price alone; September restarts and farmer fulfillment remain unknown. Conversely, H1 piracy reports improved globally and in Asia, alongside continuing Somali hijackings. [Mosaic disclosure](https://mosaicco.com/Article/Fertilizer-Markets-Facts-and-Context), [IMB H1 release](https://icc-ccs.org/lowest-first-quarter-maritime-piracy-and-armed-robbery-figures-since-1991-but-vigilance-remains-essential-2/).

## WHAT IT TOUCHES

**Food:** An income shock calls for cash, benefits and access while food remains available. A production or transport shock also needs physical stocks, alternative origins and functioning delivery. Disruption can create stranded surplus somewhere else, so the constraint must be located rather than assumed.

**Electrification:** Suitable diesel functions can migrate to electricity, reducing liquid-fuel demand while adding charging, grid, equipment and material dependencies. Start with parked refrigeration and predictable return-to-base duties. Vehicle availability is distinct from an energized depot and completed useful work. [DOE fleet guidance](https://afdc.energy.gov/vehicles/electric-fleets), [2018 food-service refrigeration example](https://afdc.energy.gov/case/3069).

**Phosphate and sulfur:** Phosphate rock supplies phosphorus; sulfur commonly supplies the acid used to process it. Industrial production mines and concentrates rock, reacts it with sulfuric acid, separates gypsum, then produces phosphoric acid and fertilizers such as MAP/DAP. Oil/sour-gas treatment can recover sulfur. If that processing contracts, useful coproduct output needs a replacement plan. This is a conditional transition issue, not an attribution of today's fertilizer constraints to electric vehicles. [EPA phosphate process](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2020-09/documents/8.9_phosphoric_acid.pdf), [EPA sulfur recovery](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2020-09/documents/8.13_sulfur_recovery.pdf).

## WHAT CAN ABSORB IT

A dependable minimum food service can combine funded purchasing, rotating appropriate stock, ready-to-eat options, water/cooking energy, paid coordination and replenishment through different producing regions and routes. Rescued food expands that floor. Reducing avoidable waste remains beneficial; preserving nutrition should not require preserving waste.

For power and transport, use demand reduction, smaller essential loads, compatible pooling and staged substitution. Test the alternate depot, supplier or neighboring region under the same shock. Two names sharing one road, grid or wholesaler are not two independent reserves.

Venezuela's heavy sour crude is a potential sulfur feedstock, but usable output needs compatible processing, sulfur recovery, handling and lawful delivery. September 14 OFAC GL52C provides a conditional pathway for specified transactions; no incremental Venezuelan merchant sulfur reserve was established. [EIA structural context, 2023](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=60762), [license text](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/936926/download?inline=).

## WHAT MAY GROW

Practical openings include food-bank purchasing cooperatives, shared storage, electrical/refrigeration maintenance, suitable vehicle conversion, nutrient efficiency/recovery, acid regeneration and community-controlled operating space. Each needs a customer, skilled work, funding and usable inputs. Announced equipment and idle buildings are not automatically available capacity.

The transition protocol records necessary function, safely removable load, released assets, replacement maturity, new dependencies, useful coproducts, worker/community effects and fallback. Healthy decline—less wasted food or diesel idling—can improve resilience when the needed service survives.

## LIFEBOATS

Proposed minimum units are a room with tested essential power, sanitation and communications; a defined food service with controlled storage and replenishment; and a modest essential-delivery function with a usable alternate. They preserve selected functions rather than reproduce the city. None of this cycle's proposed units is counted as newly commissioned.

## ISLANDS OF STABILITY

Treat stability as reciprocal exchange. California can connect local skills, institutions and food systems to the Western power system, continental logistics and global material/equipment partners. A usable offer is bounded by the donor's own commitments and contingency floor, then by delivery, compatibility and time. Western electricity exchange is real; spare imports during a common heat event are unknown. [Western market](https://www.westernenergymarkets.com/western-energy-imbalance-market-weim).

Global power matters through processing concentration, export permissions, finance and route control. The IEA's July 2026 assessment links sulfur/acid disruption to fertilizer and mineral processing. Its dated disruption account is not proof of September route closure. NATO/Commonwealth membership establishes no particular spare shipment. [IEA assessment](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2026/executive-summary).

Piracy is regional: Somali hijackings remain consequential; Asian armed robbery reports improved. A September naval response protected GLAMOR, but superiority is not constant coverage. Red Sea missile/drone conflict is analytically separate from piracy. Route safety must follow the actual cargo and current advisories. [EUNAVFOR response](https://eunavfor.eu/news/resolution-piracy-incident-gulf-aden), [regional detail](reports/RC-003/MARITIME_NETWORK.md).

## COMMONS OPPORTUNITIES

OakCLT's Liberated 23rd Avenue is a durable land-control model; RYSE Commons and West Oakland's public-asset network are existing institutional leads. Hayward and San Leandro libraries provide everyday commons functions. None is a verified vacant property or promised partner. The strongest proposal is one accessible room with keys, storage, funded hours, permission for its functions and an alternate node, pursued through a multiyear agreement or community land stewardship. [Site comparison](reports/RC-003/DURABLE_COMMONS.md).

San Anselmo was chosen because its owner documents completed microgrid construction and Town Hall solar use. It illustrates implementation, not geographic priority or tested public outage endurance. Public staffing, hours, water and backup duration remain unverified. [Town record](https://www.sananselmo.gov/1677/Town-Hall-Resilience-Hub).

Three new [commons cards](Resilience_Cascade_Commons.md) make the proposals concrete: purchased minimum nutrition, durable room control and one suitable electrified food-logistics function.

## WHAT WE MAY BE WRONG ABOUT

National food volumes do not reveal local nutritional cover. Purchasing can share the same failed upstream supply as donations. A grant is not today's free cash; a completed building is not staffed access. Reported piracy counts depend on reporting and exposure, and cannot be added across overlapping systems. Current Venezuelan spare sulfur, merchant inventories and site-level replacement readiness remain unknown.

Bayer/Monsanto is now a separate watch covering regulation/health, research integrity, governance, operations and access. EPA's updated glyphosate review remains pending; IARC's 2015 hazard classification and EPA's prior assessment are kept distinct. Corporate history warrants scrutiny but does not establish present intent. No Bayer-caused nutrient restriction was established. [EPA status](https://www.epa.gov/ingredients-used-pesticide-products/glyphosate), [IARC assessment](https://www.iarc.who.int/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/MonographVolume112-1.pdf), [watch detail](reports/RC-003/NETWORKS_AND_INPUTS.md).
