# HOMELESSNESS IS NOT A PSYCHIATRIC DIAGNOSIS.

## NEITHER IS DISOBEDIENCE.

The state cannot call coercion compassion simply because it claims to know what's best for you. The right to refuse help is part of what makes help different from control.
