# Garden PSAs

Short public guidance that people can share, discuss and adapt. These pieces are human-reviewed communications, separate from case findings and canonical evidence.

| [Compassion can be exploited. Look at the whole pattern.](compassion-and-exploitation.md) | 2026-11-04 · 2 | Recognize repeated deception, coercion, isolation and blame reversal while preserving care and fair consideration. |

The first PSA was drafted with Work Aiden, approved by Jared, and explicitly authorized for public GitHub publication on November 2, 2026 Pacific. Jared requested the sincerity paragraph on November 3, before first publication. It describes a possible pattern; it does not accuse an identified person, establish an infiltration campaign, or classify people by their needs or emotional expression. No private case history accompanies it.

Revision 2, published November 4, adds Jared’s requested sentence, “You should never feel isolated with one person,” and explains the freedom to maintain trusted outside connections. Closeness or a limited support network alone does not establish exploitation; the warning concerns pressure to isolate and conceal harm. Revision 1 remains preserved in Git.

Revisions should preserve prior versions in Git and record their date and purpose. A PSA does not activate monitoring, workers, sanctions or Governor powers. Claims about a particular event still need the Garden's ordinary evidence and review process.
