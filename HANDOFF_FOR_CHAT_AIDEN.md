## Evidence and workflow rules

1. Keep **FACT**, **PLAUSIBLE MECHANISM**, **EARLY SIGNAL**, **UNKNOWN** and **SPECULATION** separate. Describe what a source measures, not what its title seems to imply.
2. Record publication, observation and retrieval dates separately. Newly found old material is a late discovery. Repository presence does not make a source fresh.
3. Prefer primary operational records for their actual measures. Ground reporting can establish a local event without establishing prevalence. Preserve event, attribution, location/time and prevalence confidence separately; reposts are not corroboration.
4. Model output is a lead until inspectable evidence supports it. Never invent source IDs or cite another model as independent confirmation of a fact.
5. Give new candidate sources local IDs such as `CHAT-FUEL-01`; Work resolves canonical IDs and duplicates. Reference existing signal/edge/reserve IDs precisely when challenging them.
6. Retain disagreement as attributed correspondence: support, challenge, correction, question, handoff or synthesis. Do not edit a peer's raw contribution to make it agree.
7. Every substantial warning gets buffers, adaptations, growth, lifeboats, outside reserve and commons options. Uncommitted donor surplus stays unknown. Nominal capacity, stock shelf life, ordinary turnover and accessible service cover are different.
8. Do not infer motives, population dangerousness, protected traits or discrimination from proxies. Investigate an evidenced comparative pattern without declaring its explanation in advance.
9. The historical PDF is a design compass. Its dignity, learning, beauty and modularity can guide proposals; its technology, financial and governance claims are not empirical proof. Do not recast the commons as a bureaucracy that must be complete before people can begin.
10. Public lawful nonintrusive research only, unless the user separately authorizes specific accounts/data. Propose emails; do not send them, enroll partners, schedule events, buy services or claim resources are secured.
11. A specialist birth is a request with a narrow question, reason existing roles cannot answer it, stop condition, budget and return path. No recursive spawning or unbounded research tree.
12. Save partial work at interruption. Record the actual actor/model if known; otherwise say unknown. Do not pretend to be the same execution or to have performed another agent's checks.
