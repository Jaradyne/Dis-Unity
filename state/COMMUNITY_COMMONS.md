Cycle RC-001; cutoff 2026-09-20.

**id:** COM01

**name:** Braided Bridge essential-trip pilot

**cluster id:** CL01 / CL02

**status:** Reviewable proposal; no outreach, commitments or deployment

## who can act

- Two or three consenting nonprofits or neighborhood groups

## first steps

- Start with one repeat supply run and a small number of essential appointments.
- Map existing public transit first, then match usable seats/cargo windows without harming accessibility.
- Offer a draft contribution cap and mileage reimbursement approach for partner review.
- Trial only after driver, vehicle, liability and accessible-trip requirements are confirmed.

**protected floor:** Each partner retains its ordinary service commitments, driver rest and a replenishable emergency reserve. No assumed access to another organization vehicle.

**success measure:** Completed essential trips, cost per completed trip, empty miles and missed pickups; compare with baseline.

## resources

- BART/SFMTA service remains primary backbone
- ACCFB/FoodNow provides food-location leads

**Sources:** [SRC08: BART](https://www.bart.gov/sites/default/files/2026-09/FY27%20Adopted%20Budget%20Memo_FINAL_SIGNED.pdf), [SRC09: SFMTA](https://www.sfmta.com/projects/sfmta-budget-planning-fy-2026-27-and-fy-2027-28), [SRC21: Alameda County Community Food Bank](https://www.accfb.org/get-food/), [SRC24: Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/record-us-diesel-prices-squeeze-farmers-food-prices-may-rise-2026-09-18/)

**recordkeeping:** Coordinator maintains public directory and plan. A designated partner dispatcher holds minimal operational records only if pilot adopted; no client-sensitive data in public state.

**id:** COM02

**name:** Small food aggregation and cold-chain map

**cluster id:** CL01

**status:** Reviewable proposal; no outreach, commitments or deployment

## who can act

- Food bank partners, kitchens, local businesses and a nonprofit

## first steps

- Use ACCFB public food locator to seed nearby options.
- Record public pickup windows; keep proposed storage capacity unknown until operator confirmation.
- Begin with shelf-stable aggregation; add perishables only with verified temperature-safe storage and transport.
- Identify a second node on a different outage dependency before relying on one fridge.

**protected floor:** Maintain existing client allotments, host staff time and normal food safety; no speculative stockpiling.

**success measure:** Useful food delivered per trip; spoilage and missed pickups; host workload.

## resources

- Existing ACCFB referral network
- No cold-storage partner is currently committed

**Sources:** [SRC21: Alameda County Community Food Bank](https://www.accfb.org/get-food/), [SRC24: Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/record-us-diesel-prices-squeeze-farmers-food-prices-may-rise-2026-09-18/)

**id:** COM03

**name:** Repair and tools exchange

**cluster id:** CL04

**status:** Reviewable proposal; no outreach, commitments or deployment

## who can act

- Tenant association, maker space, union local or community group

## first steps

- Use Oakland Tool Lending Library eligibility and borrowing rules for eligible participants.
- Separate ordinary household repairs from work requiring qualified trades.
- Create one recurring skills-sharing session around actual repair demand.
- Use paid/voluntary capacity with explicit limits; do not assume unemployed workers are free labor.

**protected floor:** Keep tool lending access equitable; cap volunteer hours; no unqualified electrical or structural work.

**success measure:** Items safely repaired, money saved and participant time; no crisis required.

## resources

- Oakland Tool Lending Library, 5205 Telegraph Avenue; published service listing

**Sources:** [SRC20: Oakland Public Library](https://oaklandlibrary.org/otll/), [SRC13: BLS](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.nr0.htm)

**id:** COM04

**name:** One-room communications lifeboat

**cluster id:** CL04

**status:** Reviewable proposal; no outreach, commitments or deployment

## who can act

- Library, church, school or nonprofit with a willing host

## first steps

- Choose the essential function: charging/communications and public information.
- List host critical loads and measure power/duration needs before proposing hardware.
- Check a different communications path and reachable alternate location.
- Tabletop an outage with a named host and hours; mark untested functions as unavailable.

**protected floor:** Host ordinary services and safety first. Exclude life-support or clinical equipment without a responsible qualified plan.

**success measure:** Verified usable hours, accessible capacity and successful handoff to alternate site.

## resources

- West Oakland/Richmond hub projects are leads only
- No site commissioning or backup duration verified

**Sources:** [SRC22: NorCal Resilience Network](https://norcalresilience.org/resilient-hub-initiative/), [SRC10: CAISO](https://www.caiso.com/documents/2026-summer-loads-and-resources-assessment.pdf)

**id:** COM05

**name:** Small grower input-efficiency cooperative

**cluster id:** CL03

**status:** Reviewable proposal; no commitments

## who can act

- Community gardens, small farms and a cooperative extension or qualified agronomy partner

## first steps

- Share crop/soil testing costs where appropriate.
- Compare equivalent nutrient needs and public supplier options without substituting one nutrient for another.
- Pool compatible orders only when storage, timing and payment protect every member.
- Assess safe local nutrient recovery as a separate longer-term project.

**protected floor:** Protect crop requirements, safe material use and small growers cash buffers; no stockpile that burdens participants.

**success measure:** Useful nutrient delivered on time, cost per appropriate application and avoided waste.

## resources

- Qualified agronomy and current supplier evidence still to be selected

**Sources:** [SRC05: USDA ERS](https://www.ers.usda.gov/topics/farm-economy/farm-sector-income-finances/farm-sector-income-forecast), [SRC07: Nutrien](https://www.nutrien.com/news/press-releases/nutrien-reports-second-quarter-2026-results-1753)

**recordkeeping:** Coordinator maintains public evidence and proposal; operational soil/farm information stays with consenting participants.

**id:** COM06

**name:** Shared reserve and fair-allocation agreement

**cluster id:** CL05 / all clusters

**status:** Reviewable proposal; no evidence of an active local escalation cluster

## who can act

- Several nonprofits, tenant groups, library/community representatives

## first steps

- Define which essential functions each participant protects first.
- Publish simple contribution limits, access criteria and a way to request review of an allocation.
- Choose trusted facilitators and an accessible feedback channel.
- Practice an ordinary service disruption and record successful cooperation, not only conflict.

**protected floor:** No identities or political positions scored as threats; no donor pressured below its service floor.

**success measure:** Requests resolved, equitable access, preserved donor service and fewer avoidable handoff failures.

## resources

- Existing civic networks; facilitator availability unverified

**Sources:** [SRC14: FBI](https://www.fbi.gov/news/press-releases/fbi-releases-2025-reported-crimes-in-the-nation-statistics), [SRC15: CDEMA](https://www.cdema.org/index.php/cdemanews/categories/press-releases/146-press-releases/1870-cdema-launches-regional-recovery-experts-roster-to-strengthen-caribbean-recovery), [SRC22: NorCal Resilience Network](https://norcalresilience.org/resilient-hub-initiative/)

**recordkeeping:** Coordinator keeps the public agreement draft; use aggregate outcomes and no individual threat profiles.
