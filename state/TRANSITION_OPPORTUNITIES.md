# TRANSITION_OPPORTUNITIES

Cycle RC-002; cutoff 2026-09-20.

**classification:** Managed shedding / creative transition

**cluster id:** CL01

**conversion requirements:** Dispatch partner, lawful food handling, accessible trip design and measured baseline

**function to preserve:** Reliable food and essential transport

**growth candidate:** Joint routes, food aggregation, efficient suitable vehicles

**id:** TR01

**name:** Reduce fuel per useful delivery

**released assets:** Possible release of duplicate vehicle trips and dispatch time; no fleet abandonment assumed

**risks:** Consolidation can overcentralize service or lengthen vulnerable riders trips

**safe load release:** Empty miles, duplicated pickups and avoidable idling

**Sources:** [SRC01: EIA](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/), [SRC24: Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/record-us-diesel-prices-squeeze-farmers-food-prices-may-rise-2026-09-18/)

**status:** Proposed opportunity unless explicitly stated

**success observation:** Essential service improves or holds while resource burden falls; verify distributional effects.

**time horizon:** Weeks for routing; longer for equipment

**cluster id:** CL03

**conversion requirements:** Agronomy, contamination controls, processing and reliable nutrient composition

**function to preserve:** Agronomically adequate nutrition

**growth candidate:** Precision application and safe nutrient recovery

**id:** TR02

**name:** Recover nutrient value and lower avoidable inputs

**classification:** Healthy replacement/growth candidate with implementation evidence

**cluster id:** CL06

**conversion requirements:** Staffing, scheduling, maintenance, recurring operating funds, clinical availability verification

**function to preserve:** Timely local diagnostics and clinical workforce

**growth candidate:** Reliable local imaging, qualified maintenance and care navigation

**id:** TR07

**name:** Replace unreliable imaging and protect local access

**released assets:** Potential avoided transfer trips and external imaging demand; no assumed surplus land, labor or saleable old equipment

**risks:** Capital improvements do not close cash gap; partner consolidation could reduce local access if protections absent

**safe load release:** Avoid transfers solely for imaging once local service and clinical appropriateness are confirmed; no necessary care shedding

**Sources:** [RC2H01: California HCAI](https://hcai.ca.gov/facilities/health-facility-financing/distressed-hospital-loan-program/), [RC2H04: Pajaro Valley Health Care District](https://www.pvhcd.org/home-page/page/federal-cuts-put-watsonville-community-hospital-risk-partnership-way-forward), [RC2H05: Pajaro Valley Health Care District](https://www.pvhcd.org/home-page/page/new-mri-and-ct-imaging-now-available-watsonville-community-hospital)

**status:** Replacement installed/licensed; measured access benefit pending

**success observation:** Published regular service plus shorter imaging waits and fewer avoidable transfers without added staffing stress

**time horizon:** Near-term service verification after September licensing; ongoing operations

**classification:** Potential managed shedding with documented worker harm; healthy transition not established

**cluster id:** CL03

**conversion requirements:** Worker-led agreements, income protection, competent process engineering, market demand and reliable feedstock

**function to preserve:** Safe restart capability and agronomically necessary phosphate supply

**growth candidate:** Qualified maintenance, compatible alternative feedstock sourcing and nutrient recovery/efficiency

**id:** TR08

**name:** Preserve nutrient production skills through temporary idling

**released assets:** Operator-reported furloughed labor/time; no evidence that plants, equipment or capital are transferable

**risks:** Loss of skills can lengthen recovery; unsafe input substitution or uncompensated labor must not be booked as resilience

**safe load release:** Temporary uneconomic output reduction only where essential nutrient access remains protected

**Sources:** [TRS02: The Mosaic Company](https://mosaicco.com/Article/Fertilizer-Markets-Facts-and-Context)

**status:** Proposed response to observed curtailment; no partner commitments

**success observation:** Documented retention/redeployment and restored or substituted essential output without shifting unmanageable load to workers or farms

**time horizon:** Months; permanent conversion longer
