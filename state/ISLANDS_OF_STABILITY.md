# ISLANDS_OF_STABILITY

Cycle RC-001; cutoff 2026-09-20.

**id:** ISL01

**name:** Canadian potash / North American agricultural network

**Sources:** [SRC07: Nutrien](https://www.nutrien.com/news/press-releases/nutrien-reports-second-quarter-2026-results-1753), [SRC06: FAO](https://www.fao.org/worldfoodsituation/csdb/en/)

## offers

- Potash production; complementary North American crop supply

## needs in return

- Fuel, rail/ports, equipment, other nutrients and functioning payment/trade channels

**available surplus:** Not quantified; candidate complement, not committed rescue capacity

**donor guardrail:** Deduct domestic requirements, sold cargoes, maintenance and logistics reserve before offering additional tonnes

**limits:** One nutrient and one producer record do not prove whole-region independence.

**self sufficient:** False

**id:** ISL04

**name:** Caribbean mutual-aid institutions

**Sources:** [SRC15: CDEMA](https://www.cdema.org/index.php/cdemanews/categories/press-releases/146-press-releases/1870-cdema-launches-regional-recovery-experts-roster-to-strengthen-caribbean-recovery), [SRC16: CDEMA](https://www.cdema.org/index.php/cdemanews/categories/situation-reports/1852-situation-report-18-hurricane-melissa)

## offers

- Recovery expertise and demonstrated temporary clinical support

## needs in return

- Transport, funded deployment, medicines/equipment, replenishment and donor staff coverage

**available surplus:** Not quantified; candidate complement, not committed rescue capacity

**donor guardrail:** No team deployed below home essential staffing; diversify donors outside common storm footprint

**limits:** Historical medical deployment and new roster do not establish current surge strength.

**self sufficient:** False

## needs in return

**available surplus:** Not quantified; candidate complement, not committed rescue capacity
